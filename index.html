PROJECT MISSION & GOVERNANCE

About the Sentinel-Alert Platform

Engineered to combat the dangerous viral spread of synthetic, spoofed, and panic-inducing crisis rumors.

🎯 Objective & Problem Statement

During natural catastrophes and security events, unverified rumors spread 6x faster than official warnings. Panic-driven forwarding leads to stampedes, resource misallocation, and public distress. Sentinel-Alert acts as a front-line decision-support barrier to help citizens and monitors evaluate emergency claims critically before acting or amplifying.

🧠 Heuristic & Linguistic Engine

Built on a forensic linguistic architecture that scrutinizes emotional temperature, cognitive manipulation, missing institutional citations, and structural anomalies. The platform operates 100% offline in client memory without sending sensitive user inputs to third parties unless explicitly configured.

🛡️ Decision Support Boundary

This system does NOT replace civil defense commands or state meteorological agencies. It assigns probabilistic risk scores to textual artifacts to counter social engineering and disinformation patterns.

⚠️

Mandatory Life-Safety & Regulatory Disclaimer

This system does not determine whether an emergency event is actually occurring in the physical world. It analyzes message characteristics and provides an AI-based risk assessment. During real emergencies, always follow verified instructions from official authorities, local first responders, and certified disaster management services.
